# PBI-012: Journal Index + Article Detail (Editorial Quality)

## Directive
Implement the Journal editorial surface (spec §22). Create `src/pages/journal/index.astro` (prerendered, lists `getArticles()` sorted descending `publishedAt` via `ArticleCard` showing category/title/excerpt/author/published date/readingTime/related Lab/Project) and `src/pages/journal/[slug].astro` (prerendered via `getStaticPaths`, branded 404 on unknown). Article detail via `ArticleLayout.astro` renders: category, title, excerpt, author, `publishedAt`/`updatedAt`, `ReadingTime`, hero image (Astro `<Image />`, `alt` required), body through `RichContent.astro` + `Prose.astro` supporting headings/paragraphs/lists/blockquotes/images/captions/code/tables/callouts/links via Markdoc/Markdown, Related Lab/Research/Experiments/Projects block, and Previous/Next article navigation (chronologically adjacent `published` articles, hide at boundaries).
