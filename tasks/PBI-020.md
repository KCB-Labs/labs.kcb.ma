# PBI-020: SEO Package — Canonical, OG/Twitter, Schema.org, Sitemap, Robots, RSS

## Directive
Make the site discoverable and shareable. Audit and complete per-page SEO in `BaseLayout` (render `title`, `description`, `canonical` absolute via `Astro.site` = `https://labs.kcb.ma`, OG `og:title/og:description/og:image/og:url`, Twitter card, and Schema.org `Organization`/`Article`/`Person`/`SoftwareSourceCode`/`Project` only where it accurately describes the content — no fabricated data). Generate `sitemap.xml` (including `/`, `/labs/*`, `/projects/*`, `/journal/*`, `/about/*`, `/participate/*` for all published content with `lastmod` from `updatedAt`/`publishedAt`) via `@astrojs/sitemap` or custom route, `robots.txt` referencing sitemap and disallowing `/keystatic/` + `/api/`, and Journal RSS (`/journal/rss.xml` or `/rss.xml`) from `getArticles()` sorted descending `publishedAt`. Ensure `/keystatic` and `/api/*` are excluded from indexing. Keep SEO strings derived from collection `seoTitle`/`seoDescription`/`socialImage` with sensible fallbacks, not hard-coded page literals.
